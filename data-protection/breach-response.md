# Personal Data Breach — Response & Notification

**Project:** AI-Assisted Education System for Government Schools in Karnataka (IIT Dharwad × DSEL)
**Version:** 1.0 (2026-06-27) — draft, pending DPO/legal review
**References:** DPDP Act 2023 & DPDP Rules 2025 (breach notification); MoU Clause 9

---

## 1. What counts as a breach

Any unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction of personal data — e.g. leak of unredacted scans, theft/loss of a device or backup, exposed image URLs, or an annotator copying/sharing data.

## 2. Roles under DPDP

- **IIT Dharwad (Processor):** detect, contain, and **report to the Department without undue delay**; assist with the Department's notification obligations.
- **DSEL (Fiduciary):** notify the **Data Protection Board** and **affected data principals** as required by the DPDP Rules 2025.

## 3. Procedure

1. **Detect & record** — log the incident (what happened, when discovered, data/individuals affected, likely cause).
2. **Contain** — revoke access, rotate credentials/keys, take affected services offline if needed.
3. **Assess** — scope and severity; whether unredacted PII or only anonymised data is involved.
4. **Escalate internally** — notify the project data-protection contact and IITDH DPO immediately.
5. **Notify the Department** — inform the DSEL nodal officer **without undue delay** (target: within 24–72 hours of becoming aware), with the facts and remediation taken/planned.
6. **Remediate** — fix the root cause; apply preventive measures.
7. **Close & review** — record outcome and lessons; update `security-measures.md` if needed.
