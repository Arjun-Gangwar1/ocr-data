# Annotator Confidentiality & Data-Handling Undertaking

**Project:** AI-Assisted Education System for Government Schools in Karnataka
**Institution:** Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad
**Version:** 1.0 (2026-06-27)

*Complements the engagement/payment terms in the annotation pipeline contracts. Every person who accesses project data signs this before being given access.*

---

I, **[NAME]** ( **[role / ID]** ), confirm that I have read and agree to the following in respect of the student answer-script data of the above project:

1. **Confidentiality.** All answer scripts and related data are confidential and the property of the Government of Karnataka. I will not disclose them to anyone outside the authorised project team.

2. **Access only via the platform.** I will access data only through the project's annotation platform, using my own account. I will not download, copy, photograph, screenshot, print, or remove any image or data from IITDH systems.

3. **Work only on assigned, redacted material.** I will work only on the pages assigned to me. Student names and roll numbers are masked; I will **not attempt to read, recover, transcribe, or record** any identifying information, and will not record it in any annotation.

4. **Report issues.** If I encounter any unmasked personal information, a suspected data leak, or any security concern, I will report it to the project data-protection contact immediately and not act further on that page.

5. **Devices & environment.** I will keep my account credentials secure, lock my screen when away, and not access the data on shared/public devices or networks outside what the project permits.

6. **Use limitation.** I will use the data solely for annotation tasks assigned under this project, and for no other purpose.

7. **Survival.** These obligations continue after my engagement ends. On ending my engagement, I will retain no copies of any data.

8. **Consequences.** I understand that breach of this undertaking may result in termination and, where applicable, action under law.
